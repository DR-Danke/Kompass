## ROI Calculation

### 1. Sales Team Productivity (Largest Driver)

The core problem: sales reps doing data entry instead of selling.

```
Monthly admin cost = Sales team x Work hours/mo x Hourly cost x % time on admin
Example: 4 reps x 160hrs x $18/hr x 0.65 = $7,488/mo wasted on admin
```

With Kompass (reduce admin to 25%):
```
New admin cost = 4 x 160 x $18 x 0.25 = $2,880/mo
Savings = $7,488 - $2,880 = $4,608/mo
```

### 2. Quotation Volume & Revenue Impact

Faster quotations = more quotations = more deals:

```
Current: 2 quotes/week × 4 weeks = 8 quotes/month
With Kompass: 12 quotes/week × 4 weeks = 48 quotes/month

Additional quotes = 40/month
Additional deals = 40 × 25% conversion = 10 deals/month
Additional revenue = 10 × $15,000 avg = $150,000/month
Additional margin = $150,000 × 15% margin = $22,500/month
```

Even conservatively (3x quotation increase instead of 6x):
```
Additional monthly margin = $7,500/mo
```

### 3. Quote Turnaround & Win Rate

First-to-quote advantage: clients often award business to the first quote received.

```
Current turnaround: 1-3 days
With Kompass: 15 minutes

Win rate improvement: +10-15% on competitive deals
On $300,000/month in quoted business:
Additional wins = $300,000 × 0.10 × 0.15 margin = $4,500/mo
```

### 4. Trade Fair ROI Amplification

Without Kompass: 200 cards collected, 20 followed up (10%), rest forgotten.
With Kompass: 200 cards captured, 180 extracted and auto-followed-up (90%):

```
Additional supplier relationships = 160 more contacts followed up
Conversion to active supplier = 10% = 16 new suppliers
Revenue from new suppliers (year 1) = 16 × $20,000 = $320,000/yr → $26,667/mo
Margin = $26,667 × 0.15 = $4,000/mo
```

### 5. Product Catalog Building Speed

```
Manual: 100 products × 5 min each = 8.3 hours
AI extraction: 100 products in 30 minutes

Time saved per catalog import: ~8 hours × $18/hr = $144
Monthly catalog imports: 3-5 supplier catalogs
Monthly savings = 4 × $144 = $576/mo
```

### 6. Pricing Consistency & Error Prevention

Manual Excel calculations lead to pricing errors (wrong HS code, outdated exchange rate, forgotten tariff):

```
Pricing errors per month: 2-3 quotations with incorrect pricing
Cost per error (margin erosion or deal loss): $500-2,000
Monthly cost = 2.5 × $1,000 = $2,500/mo

With automated pricing engine: near-zero errors
Savings = $2,500/mo
```
