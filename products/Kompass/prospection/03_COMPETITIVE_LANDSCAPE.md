# Competitive Landscape — Portfolio & Quotation Automation for China Sourcing

## Direct Competitors

### Alibaba.com (Gold Supplier Tools)
- **Focus**: Supplier discovery and direct sourcing platform
- **Pricing**: $1,500-6,000/yr for Gold Supplier membership
- **Strengths**: Massive supplier database, RFQ system, trade assurance, payment escrow
- **Weakness**: Marketplace model — focused on supplier-buyer matching, not the trading company's internal operations. No catalog management, no quotation generation, no import cost calculation. Competing with your own suppliers on the platform
- **Our edge**: Kompass manages the trading company's own operations — their catalog, their pricing, their client relationships. Alibaba is where you find suppliers; Kompass is where you sell to your clients

### TradeGecko / QuickBooks Commerce
- **Focus**: Inventory management and multi-channel order management
- **Pricing**: $39-199/mo (before Intuit acquisition)
- **Strengths**: Inventory tracking, multi-channel sales, purchase orders, basic B2B quoting
- **Weakness**: No AI product extraction. No business card capture. No import cost calculator with HS codes and Colombian tariffs. Not built for China-sourcing trading companies
- **Our edge**: Purpose-built for the sourcing-to-quotation workflow. AI extraction, import cost engine, supplier certification

### Zoho Inventory / Zoho CRM
- **Focus**: All-in-one business suite with inventory and CRM
- **Pricing**: $59-239/mo (Inventory) + $14-52/user/mo (CRM)
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive suite, good value, strong automation
- **Weakness**: Generic — not built for trading companies. No AI catalog extraction. No HS code / tariff calculation. No trade fair features. Per-user pricing scales quickly
- **Our edge**: Vertical-specific features (business card capture, audit certification, import cost calculation) that generic tools can't match

### HubSpot Sales Hub
- **Focus**: CRM, deal pipeline, document generation
- **Pricing**: $45-120/user/mo
- **Strengths**: Excellent CRM, strong email automation, deal tracking, document templates
- **Weakness**: No product catalog management. No import cost calculations. No AI extraction. Per-user pricing prohibitive for sales teams
- **Our edge**: Product catalog with AI extraction, quotation engine with import cost calculations, trade fair integration

### PandaDoc
- **Focus**: Document generation, e-signatures, proposals
- **Pricing**: $19-49/user/mo
- **Strengths**: Beautiful document templates, e-signatures, content library, analytics
- **Weakness**: Document tool, not a business tool. No product catalog. No pricing engine. No supplier or client management. No AI extraction
- **Our edge**: Full pipeline from supplier management through product catalog to quotation — PandaDoc is just the document generation step

### CamCard / ABBYY Business Card Reader
- **Focus**: Business card scanning and contact extraction
- **Pricing**: $5-15/user/mo
- **Strengths**: Fast card scanning, CRM sync, good recognition accuracy
- **Weakness**: Card scanning only — no supplier management, no certification, no follow-up automation, no product pipeline
- **Our edge**: Card capture is one step in the pipeline — cards become suppliers, suppliers become products, products become quotations

## Indirect Competitors

### Excel + WhatsApp + Email (Status Quo)
- **The real competitor** — 80%+ of China-sourcing trading companies in LATAM
- Product catalogs in Excel, quotes in Word, business cards in a box, client comms on WhatsApp
- Advantages: Free, familiar, flexible
- Disadvantages: Sales team spends 75% on data entry, quote turnaround takes days, business cards pile up, no supplier certification trail, no pricing consistency
- **Displacement strategy**: Demonstrate the time cost ($7,200-14,400/month for 4-person team) and the revenue lost from slow quotation turnaround

### WeChat + Spreadsheets (Supplier Communication)
- Trading companies manage supplier relationships through WeChat and track products in spreadsheets
- **Weakness**: No structured data, no searchable catalog, supplier info trapped in chat history
- **Our edge**: WeChat integration that captures cards and messages into a structured database

### Custom-Built Internal Tools
- Some larger trading companies build internal catalog/quotation systems
- **Weakness**: Expensive to build and maintain, no AI features, single-developer dependency
- **Our edge**: AI-powered extraction, professionally maintained, fraction of development cost

## Our Unique Differentiators

### 1. AI-Powered Trade Fair Pipeline (Primary)
No competitor integrates business card capture → AI extraction → auto-supplier creation → follow-up email → WeChat integration into a single workflow. At Canton Fair (30,000+ exhibitors), this is the difference between following up with 20 suppliers and following up with 200.

### 2. AI Factory Audit Analysis
Upload a 70-page factory audit PDF and get automated extraction of 12+ data points with A/B/C certification recommendation. No other product does AI analysis of factory audit documents for supplier qualification.

### 3. AI Product Catalog Extraction
Upload a supplier's PDF catalog, Excel price list, or even product images → get structured product data extracted automatically. The biggest time sink in trading company operations (8 hours for 100 products manually vs. 30 minutes with AI).

### 4. Colombian Import Cost Calculator
Full pricing engine: FOB + tariffs (from HS codes) + freight + inspection + insurance × exchange rate + national freight + nationalization + margin. This is the specific calculation every Colombian importer needs to do on every quotation — built into the quotation engine, not done in a separate spreadsheet.

### 5. Supplier-to-Quotation Pipeline
The complete vertical workflow: trade fair capture → supplier certification → product catalog → curated portfolio → client CRM → quotation with import cost → proforma PDF → email delivery → tracking link. No competitor covers this full pipeline.

### 6. WeChat Integration
Send and receive messages through WeChat Official Account — the primary communication channel with Chinese suppliers. No Western CRM or quoting tool integrates with WeChat.

- 3-15 person companies importing Chinese goods for Colombian/LATAM clients

- **Why us**: Purpose-built for their exact workflow, AI extraction saves massive time, import cost calculator built in

- Similar companies in Peru, Ecuador, Mexico, Chile sourcing from China
- Same fundamental workflow: suppliers → products → quotations
- **Why us**: Same value proposition with localizable import cost calculations per country

### Tertiary: Sourcing Agents / Buying Offices
- Intermediaries helping multiple clients source from China
- Need to manage multiple client catalogs and quotations
- **Why us**: Multi-portfolio management, client-specific catalogs, white-label quotations

### Future: Manufacturing Purchasing Departments
- Colombian manufacturers importing components/raw materials from China
- Need supplier certification and import cost tracking
- **Why us**: Supplier audit certification, HS code management, import cost tracking at scale
