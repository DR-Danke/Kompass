# Kompass — Pricing Strategy

## Product Overview

AI-powered portfolio and quotation automation platform for China-sourcing trading companies. Covers the complete sales cycle: supplier discovery at trade fairs (AI business card extraction), supplier certification (AI audit analysis), product catalog management (AI catalog import), curated portfolio creation, client CRM pipeline, and automated quotation generation with Colombian import cost calculations (FOB + tariffs + freight + nationalization + margin).

### Core Value Proposition

A China-sourcing trading company's sales team spends **75% of their time on non-selling activities**: manually entering products from supplier catalogs, building quotes in Excel, calculating import costs, formatting proformas, and managing contacts on business cards and WhatsApp. At $15-30/hr fully loaded, a 4-person sales team wastes **$7,200-14,400/month** on data entry instead of selling.

Kompass flips the ratio: **75% selling, 25% admin** — through AI-powered automation at every step.

Key metrics:
- Extract 100 products from catalog: **8 hours → 30 minutes** (94% reduction)
- Generate a quotation: **1 day → 15 minutes** (98% reduction)
- Quotations per week: **1-2 → 10-15** (5-10x increase)

No competitor offers AI factory audit analysis, automated import cost calculations (FOB + tariffs + freight + nationalization), or the supplier-to-quotation pipeline as a single product.
