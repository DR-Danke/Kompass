# First Customer Playbook — Kompass

## Recommended Offer

**$0 launch fee + $199/mo** (3-month pilot)

### Why This Price

- **No launch fee** removes the biggest objection. The product needs real supplier and product data to demonstrate its full value — charging for setup before proving the workflow is premature
- **$199/mo is less than one day** of a sales rep's time. If it saves even 2 days/month of data entry, it's already profitable for them
- **Not free** — $199 ensures the company actually commits to loading their real product catalog and running real quotations through the platform
- **3-month commitment** covers at least one trade fair cycle or one full sales quarter — enough to prove the end-to-end pipeline

### What to Ask in Return

In exchange for the launch pricing:

1. **Real catalog data** — import at least 100 products from actual supplier catalogs into the platform
2. **Trade fair usage** — use the business card capture feature at their next trade fair (Canton Fair, Yiwu, etc.)
3. **Quotation flow** — generate and send at least 10 real quotations to clients through the platform
4. **Weekly feedback** — 15-min call or written notes on extraction accuracy, pricing accuracy, and workflow fit
5. **Case study permission** — allow anonymous or named usage for marketing
6. **Reference availability** — willing to speak with 1-2 prospective companies

---

## Ideal First Customer Profile

- **China-sourcing trading company** based in Colombia importing 20-100+ products for LATAM clients
- **3-10 person sales team** currently managing product catalogs in Excel and quotes in Word/PDF manually
- **Attends China trade fairs** (Canton Fair, Yiwu) at least once per year
- **Manages 20-50 suppliers** across different product categories
- **Pain points**: Product catalog is scattered across PDFs and spreadsheets, quotations take too long, business cards pile up after trade fairs, no visibility into supplier certification quality
- **Decision maker**: Commercial director or owner who sees the sales team bottleneck
- **Product types**: Physical goods with HS codes and import duty (not services or digital)

### Red Flags (Avoid as First Customer)

- Company that imports only 1-2 products (not enough volume to justify the platform)
- Customer who expects the platform to replace their accounting/ERP system
- Company that doesn't attend trade fairs and doesn't need the card capture feature
- Very large distributor with complex multi-currency, multi-warehouse requirements beyond quotation

## Onboarding Checklist (First Customer)

### Week 1: Foundation Setup

- [ ] Create company profile with branding for proformas
- [ ] Set up user accounts (sales team + commercial director)
- [ ] Define product category taxonomy (aligned to their product lines)
- [ ] Import HS codes for their primary product categories
- [ ] Configure pricing settings (exchange rate, margin %, inspection, insurance, nationalization)
- [ ] Set up freight rates for their common routes (Shenzhen/Shanghai/Yiwu → Buenaventura/Cartagena)
- [ ] Walk through the full workflow end-to-end

### Week 2: Catalog Building

- [ ] AI-extract products from 2-3 existing supplier catalogs (PDF/Excel)
- [ ] Review extracted products, correct any errors, import to catalog
- [ ] Manually add 20-30 key products with images and detailed specs
- [ ] Set up categories and tags for organization
- [ ] Import supplier directory from existing CRM/spreadsheets
- [ ] First feedback session — focus on extraction accuracy

### Week 3-4: Sales Workflow

- [ ] Create first curated portfolio for a specific client/niche
- [ ] Generate PDF portfolio and share link
- [ ] Create and send first quotation with full import cost calculation
- [ ] Test quotation email delivery and client tracking link
- [ ] Set up outreach email templates
- [ ] Import client list with pipeline status
- [ ] Second feedback session — focus on quotation accuracy and workflow

### Month 2-3: Full Adoption

- [ ] Sales team creating all quotations through Kompass
- [ ] Product catalog growing through AI extraction
- [ ] Client pipeline managed in CRM Kanban board
- [ ] If trade fair during period: use business card capture extensively
- [ ] Portfolio shares generating client engagement
- [ ] Quotation volume at 5-10x previous manual rate
- [ ] Collect metrics for case study

## Success Metrics to Track

For the case study and to justify price increases:

1. **Quotation throughput**: Quotes/week before vs. after (target: 5-10x increase)
2. **Quote turnaround time**: Time from client request to proforma delivery (target: 15 min vs. 1 day)
3. **Catalog building speed**: Products added/week via AI extraction vs. manual entry
4. **Business card processing**: Cards captured and extracted per trade fair day (target: 30-50/day vs. 10-15 manual)
5. **Pipeline conversion**: Quote-to-deal conversion rate improvement (from faster turnaround)
6. **Sales team utilization**: % of time on selling vs. admin (target: 75% selling vs. 25% prior)
7. **Supplier coverage**: Number of certified suppliers in system vs. prior paper-based records
